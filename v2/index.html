How to get a price.

Most jobs can be priced from photos, so you don’t have to wait in for someone to come and look.

  1. 01

    Take photos

    Of the lawn, the hedge, the gutters, whatever needs doing. Text them to 0432 036 898 or add them to the form below.

  2. 02

    Get a price

    Luke looks at the photos and sends you a price. If he can’t tell from the photos, he’ll come out and have a look.

  3. 03

    Pick a day

    Book it once, or put it on a regular round. You don’t need to be home for outdoor jobs.

  4. 04

    Cleared away

    Clippings, branches and rubbish go on the trailer and leave with us.
